About This Gallery

What does belonging look like on a campus map?

The maps collected here were created by members of the UAlbany community participating in our ongoing research project on belonging. Some are literal, some abstract, and some deeply personal—but together they begin to show the many different ways people find connection, inclusion, comfort, and a sense of feeling “at home” on campus. We will continue adding new maps here as the project grows.
